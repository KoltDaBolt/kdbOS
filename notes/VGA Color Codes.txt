0x0 - Black
0x1 - Blue
0x2 - Green
0x3 - Cyan
0x4 - Red
0x5 - Magenta
0x6 - Brown
0x7 - Light Gray - This is the background color limit
0x8 - Dark Gray
0x9 - Light Blue
0xA - Light Green
0xB - Light Cyan
0xC - Light Red
0xD - Light Magenta
0xE - Yellow
0xF - White

0xB8000 - Address used by VGA for video stuff

First four bits = foreground color
Next three bits = background color
Final bit = blinking

So for blinking blue text on a light gray background, you would put the following:
0xF1 -> 11110001 -> (0001 = blue text) + (111 = light gray background) + (1 = blinking)
